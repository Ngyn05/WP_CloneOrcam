For Reading & Learning


OrCam Learn Basic is tailored for people facing reading and learning challenges, including Dyslexia and ADHD.
Listen to any text with our Read Aloud feature (OCR and Text-To-Speech) to be your accessible reading companion.

Read and Learn Without Limits

Read Without Limits OrCam Learn Basic offers personalised reading support for Users in the classroom and at home. Powered by award-winning technology, OrCam Learn Basic is your AI assistive companion that empowers you to read and learn at your own pace, in any environment.

Listen To Any Text

Point & click, and OrCam Learn Basic will read a full page, paragraph, or single word out loud from any digital or printed surface. Listen to textbooks and worksheets easily.
