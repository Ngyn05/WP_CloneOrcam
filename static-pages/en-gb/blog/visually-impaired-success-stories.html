The Problem: A World Beyond Reach


Before discovering the OrCam Read 3, visually impaired individuals like Ashley Mizell often faced insurmountable challenges. The world of printed text and documents was largely inaccessible, making everyday tasks and academic pursuits a constant struggle.


Empowering Independence with OrCam Read 3

OrCam Read 3 changes the game. With its remarkable capabilities, this device provides real-time assistance to people with visual impairments, empowering them to access information independently. Here's how it works:

  • Reading Pen: OrCam Read 3 acts as your personal reading assistant, instantly converting printed text into spoken words. Simply point the device at a book, magazine, or any printed material, and it will read it aloud to you. No need for a separate reading device or assistance from others.
  • Magnifier: The OrCam Read 3 also functions as a magnifier, allowing users to zoom in on text or objects and view them clearly. It's like having a magnifying glass at your fingertips, providing enhanced clarity and detail.
  • Reading Stand: Need a stable surface to read or work? The OrCam Read 3 can be transformed into a reading stand, ensuring a comfortable reading experience.

Ashley herself speaks of its impact, saying, "The OrCam Read 3 has definitely changed my life for the better. It's given me so much independence and confidence."
