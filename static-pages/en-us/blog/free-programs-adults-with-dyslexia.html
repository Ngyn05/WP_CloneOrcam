​Gemm Learning's Fast ForWord: This is an adaptive online reading program that uses brain-based software with remote supervision. The program is particularly effective as it adapts to the user's pace and focuses on specific weaknesses. Multi-sensory programs

​

Multi-sensory learning techniques have been proven to be particularly effective for adult learners with dyslexia. These programs reinforce reading and spelling skills through a combination of audio, visual, and tactile learning, catering to different learning styles and satisfying various sensory needs.


The Orton-Gillingham approach is a multi-sensory teaching approach that acts as an umbrella under which several systems and methods fall. Two popular programs are the Barton program and the Wilson Reading System. These programs offer instruction that emphasizes phonics, vocabulary, and spelling rules through multi-sensory learning techniques.


The Barton program is an intensive program that comprises 10 levels, with an assessment at the end of each level to ensure that students have mastered the requisite skills before progressing to the next level. It is a highly structured program that incorporates visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learning techniques.


Similarly, the Wilson Reading System is a multi-sensory program that utilizes audio, visual, and tactile learning techniques to teach word recognition, decoding, and spelling. The program requires a one-on-one teacher-student setting and is suitable for adults with dyslexia who need a structured and individualized approach to learning.


The Davis Program, based on the book The Gift of Dyslexia written by Ron Davis, consists of activities that cater to individual motivation for learning and include the use of physical objects and symbols to represent concepts. This program is unique in that it recognizes the value of dyslexic thinking and gives students the tools to develop their strengths, such as their vivid imaginations and creativity.


Overall, multi-sensory learning programs like the Orton-Gillingham approach, the Barton program, the Wilson Reading System, and the Davis Program can be transformative for adult learners with dyslexia. These programs offer a structured and personalized approach to learning that caters to different learning styles, reinforces essential reading and spelling skills, and instills confidence in learners.
