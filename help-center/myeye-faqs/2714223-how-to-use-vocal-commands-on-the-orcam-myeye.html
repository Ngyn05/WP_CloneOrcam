How to use Vocal Commands on the OrCam MyEye

FAQs

Please see the tutorial video on how to use Vocal Commands  on the OrCam MyEye below:

 

To use the Vocal Commands: 


 

  1. Double-tap the touch bar. You will hear a long beep.
  2. Say a vocal command. You will then hear a chime.
  3. OrCam MyEye will then perform the requested action.

 

 


In addition to the traditional double-tap of the touch bar, you may simply say, "Hey OrCam", followed by the desired vocal command.
For example, you can say, "Hey OrCam, enter the user menu".
