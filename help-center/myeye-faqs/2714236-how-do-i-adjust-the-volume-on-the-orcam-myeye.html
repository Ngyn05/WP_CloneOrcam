How do I adjust the volume on the OrCam MyEye?

FAQs

There are numerous ways to control the volume on your OrCam MyEye:

 Swiping.

  • To  increase the volume by one level by swiping your finger forward along the touch bar, in the direction of the camera.
  • To decrease the volume by one level by swiping your finger backward along the touch bar, in the direction of the speaker.
  • OrCam MyEye will announce whether the volume level was raised or lowered, as well as when you reach the minimum or maximum volume level.

Note: You cannot change the volume while the device is reading. Performing the swipe action during reading will cause the device to read either the next or previous sentence.




Vocal Commands.

  • Double-tap the touch bar. You will hear a long beep.
  • Say "Volume Up" or " Volume Down"
  • OrCam MyEye will then adjust the volume accordingly by one level.


​​​​​​Hey OrCam

  • In addition to the traditional double-tap of the touch bar, you may simply say, "Hey OrCam, Volume Up" or "Hey OrCam, Volume Down".
  • OrCam MyEye will then adjust the volume accordingly by one level.
