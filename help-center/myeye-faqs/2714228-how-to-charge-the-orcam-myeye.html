How to charge the OrCam MyEye

FAQs

Please see the tutorial video on how to charge the OrCam MyEye below:

Note:  Your OrCam MyEye may arrive with the mini magnetic piece already inserted. In this case, to charge, bring the smaller end of the charging cable towards the rounded end of the device, and the cable will magnetically attach to the already inserted mini magnetic piece. The mini magnetic piece remains inserted in the device. 

The OrCam MyEye needs to be charged for at least 3 hours before first use. Subsequent charges take approximately 1 hour to charge from 0-100%

To charge the OrCam MyEye:

  1. Make sure the USB magnetic adaptor piece is already installed in the micro-USB inlet. If it is not, attach the USB magnetic adaptor piece to the micro-USB inlet on the bottom of OrCam MyEye.
  2. Connect the OrCam magnetic charging cable to the USB magnetic adaptor piece.
  3. Plug the USB end of the magnetic charging cable into the wall charger.
  4. Plug the wall charger into an electrical outlet.
