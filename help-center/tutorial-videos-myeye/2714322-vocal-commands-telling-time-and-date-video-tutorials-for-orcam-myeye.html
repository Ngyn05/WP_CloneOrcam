Video 1: Vocal Commands using the Double Tap

To get the video tutorial to play, click on the video image below. 

There are 2 options for using vocal commands:

1. Double tap the touch bar, wait for the long beep, then say the vocal command. After 2-3 seconds, you will hear a chime and the device will respond.

NOTE: The double tap for using vocal commands needs to be quick. When you do it correctly, you'll hear a long beep. If the taps are too slow, you'll hear the shutter sound of device taking a picture. If you find the double tap challenging, use the 'Hey OrCam' option (more details in the video below).

2. Say 'Hey OrCam' followed by the vocal command. After 2-3 seconds, you will hear a chime and the device will respond (more details in the video below).

Vocal commands using the double tap:

  1. Double-tap the touch bar. You will hear a long beep.
  2. Say a vocal command. You will then hear a chime.
  3. OrCam MyEye will then perform the requested action.

Video 2: Vocal Commands using 'Hey OrCam'

To get the video tutorial to play, click on the video image below. 

 

The 'Hey OrCam' feature lets you access vocal commands 'hands-free'!

Simply say, "Hey OrCam", followed by the desired vocal command.


For example, you can say, "Hey OrCam, enter the user menu".
(The image below shows a line drawing of a user saying "Hey OrCam, enter the user menu").
 
 

Link to the list of vocal commands:

Listed below are some great vocal commands to try out. Click here to view a list of the Vocal Commands for the OrCam MyEye. 

  • Tell the time
  • Tell the date
  • Tell battery status
  • Volume up
  • Volume down
  • Read slower
  • Read faster
  • Change the voice
  • Tell the device serial number
